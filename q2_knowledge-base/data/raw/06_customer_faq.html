Customer Support FAQ

How quickly are emergency calls handled?

Gold and Platinum customers receive an initial response within 2 business hours. Silver customers receive an initial response within 4 business hours.

Can a technician arrive outside normal hours?

Yes, if the contract includes after-hours coverage. Check the account record before confirming.

Who can approve a contract exception?

Regional Operations Managers may approve operational exceptions up to 25,000 INR. Finance approval is required for reimbursement exceptions.

Is the FAQ always current?

This page is maintained by Customer Operations. Contract-specific terms override general FAQ guidance.
